It picks the best one.
You just nod.

Ten photos of the cat, the same plate, the same sunset. Keep One puts photos of the same moment together and picks the best one for you.

Tap to keep it, or tap another. The rest wait until you clean up, all at once. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

On your iPhone only

Your photos are analyzed on this iPhone. Nothing is uploaded. There is no account and no photo cloud. Privacy

Free and Pro

Free: 10 groups a day. Pro adds unlimited groups, accepting every pick at once, and sorting by space to free. Terms
