Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

The short version. Your photos are analyzed on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded. Keep One has no account, no server and no photo cloud. We don’t collect personal data, we don’t use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we don’t sell anything about you. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Your photos

Keep One asks for access to your photo library so it can find photos of the same moment and pick the best one. It works only on small preview images (thumbnails), never on your originals, and the analysis runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device frameworks. Photos, previews and the results of the analysis are never sent anywhere.

If you open a photo full screen and its original is stored in iCloud Photos, your iPhone downloads it from Apple, exactly as the Photos app would. Keep One does not receive or keep a copy.

You can choose full access or selected photos only, and change it at any time in iOS Settings → Keep One → Photos.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The analysis of each photo (a numeric description used to compare photos, and quality scores), so the next scan only looks at new photos
  • The groups, which photo you kept in each, and which groups you skipped
  • When you choose a different photo than the one picked for you, Keep One adjusts how it picks — on your iPhone only
  • How many groups you confirmed today (the free limit) and your settings

All of this is stored inside the app on your iPhone. It is not synced to iCloud or anywhere else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Deleting photos

When you clean up, Keep One asks Photos to delete the photos you didn’t keep. iOS asks you to confirm, and deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Favorites are never removed.

What we never collect

  • Your photos, previews, or anything about what is in them
  • Your name, email address, contacts or location
  • Usage analytics or crash reports — Keep One contains no analytics or crash reporting SDK
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) or any cross-app or cross-site tracking. Keep One shows no ads.

Purchases

Keep One Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The app checks with Apple on your iPhone whether Pro is active. Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Feedback emails

If you send feedback from Settings → Feedback, your mail app sends an email to us. Before it opens, Keep One shows you the whole email. Besides what you write, it adds only the app version, iOS version and iPhone model. No photos or photo information are attached. We receive your email address because you sent the email, and we use the email only to reply to you. Ask us at any time and we will delete it.

Who we are

Keep One is made by Xin Yin in Singapore, who decides how the data described here is used (the “controller”). Because Keep One collects no personal data, the only personal data that can reach us is a feedback email you choose to send. For anything about your data, contact sgbusiness046@gmail.com.

Children

Keep One is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If Keep One ever starts to collect data, we will say so here and in the app before it does.

一句话版本。照片只在你的 iPhone 上分析，不会上传。Keep One 没有账户、没有服务器、也没有照片云。我们不收集个人数据，不使用任何分析、崩溃上报、广告或追踪 SDK，也不出售任何与你有关的信息。购买由 Apple 处理。

你的照片

Keep One 需要访问你的照片图库，才能找出同一时刻拍的照片并挑出最好的一张。它只使用小尺寸的预览图（缩略图），从不读取原图；分析完全在你的 iPhone 上用 Apple 的本机框架完成。照片、预览图和分析结果都不会发送到任何地方。

你可以选择允许访问全部照片或只允许选中的照片，并随时在 iOS 设置 → Keep One → 照片 里更改。

Keep One Pro 通过 App Store 销售，由 Apple 处理付款；我们看不到你的付款信息，也看不到你的 Apple 账户。App 在你的 iPhone 上向 Apple 确认 Pro 是否有效。管理或取消订阅：iOS 设置 → 你的姓名 → 订阅。
