  • Free: scanning, grouping, seeing every group and the picks, and confirming 10 groups a day. No payment required.
  • Keep One Pro: unlimited groups, accepting all picks at once, and sorting by space to free.

Pro is available as an annual subscription (US$19.99 / year, with a 3-day free trial for eligible Apple Accounts), a weekly subscription (US$2.99 / week, no trial), or a one-time purchase (US$29.99). These are US prices; the App Store shows your local price before you confirm.

Billing & renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. For the annual plan with a trial, the yearly price is charged when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and the renewal is charged within 24 hours before the period ends. The one-time purchase does not renew.

Cancellation

Cancelling a subscription keeps Pro available until the end of the period you paid for. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Restore purchases on a new iPhone from Settings → Restore purchases in the app.

Your photos

Keep One only deletes photos after you confirm in the iOS deletion prompt. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. You decide what is deleted; please check before you confirm.
